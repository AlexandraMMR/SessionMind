## AI That Pays Off: Starting Smart, Scaling Right and Optimizing Costs (sponsored by CGI Inc.)
*AIM102-S · 100 – Foundational · Expo, Athena Theater*

Most AI initiatives stall before they show any value—or worse, generate massive cloud bills without delivering ROI. In this session, CGI shares how technical leaders can set up AI efforts for success from day one. We’ll walk through our AI Lab accelerator, built for rapid experimentation and responsible implementation, with governance, risk controls, and standards that get you to production faster.
 
Then we’ll dive into a real-world case where our FinOps model helped a client cut $2 million—nearly 50%—from their cloud spend by applying a structured approach to cost governance and financial accountability. In particular, we’ll explore how FinOps plays a critical role in managing the financial risks commonly associated with high-performance GPU workloads that power AI. From tagging strategies and usage forecasting to automated anomaly detection and chargeback models, you’ll see how CGI helps clients stay in control while scaling AI responsibly.
 
Whether you're just getting started or already scaling, this session will help you move faster, spend smarter, and adopt better. This presentation is brought to you by CGI Inc., an AWS Partner.
